The Game

How Fantasy Fights Works

Three simple steps between you and bragging rights over your friends.

01
📋

Join a League & Draft Your Team

Create or join a league with up to 8 managers. Enter the live snake draft room and pick 20 real professional boxers — 4 fighters across each of 5 weight divisions — using a 90-second pick timer. Strategy matters: do you stack the heavyweight division or spread across all weights?

02
🥊

Your Fighters Enter the Ring

Sit back as real fights happen around the world. Every time one of your drafted fighters competes, they score points for your team. Wins, knockouts, championship defences — each fight result updates your Fantasy Fights roster in real time. No simulation. Real boxing. Real stakes.

03
🏆

Score Points & Climb the Standings

Watch your team climb the leaderboard as your boxers rack up wins. Head-to-head weekly matchups, season-long standings, and championship tournaments separate the scouts from the pretenders. The manager who best predicted boxing's elite takes the title.

The Points System

Simple. Transparent. Fight-Driven.

Every point comes directly from real fight outcomes. No arbitrary scoring algorithms — just boxing results.

3 PTS

Victory

Your fighter wins the bout by any method — decision, TKO, KO, or DQ

+1 BONUS

Knockout Bonus

Extra point awarded when your fighter stops their opponent by KO or TKO

1 EACH

Championship Belts

Ongoing points for every major title belt held — IBF, WBA, WBC, WBO

1 EACH

BoxRec Star Rating

Points for each BoxRec star earned — rewards drafting recognised elite fighters

Our Journey

Four Years Forged in Bondi

Fantasy Fights isn't a concept. It's a proven product, battle-tested across four full seasons.

2021

Born in Bondi

4 mates, a boxing obsession, and an Excel spreadsheet. The first Fantasy Fights season runs manually over WhatsApp.

2022

League Expansion

7 teams. Head-to-head matchups introduced. 21 fighters per team. The concept proves its legs — everyone wants back in.

2023

8 Teams & Website

League grows to 8 managers. Refined to 20 fighters per team. fantasyfights.live launches. Product leaves Excel for good.

2024

App Development

React Native app enters active development. Draft engine, live scoring, and league management built from the ground up.

2026

Season 1 Global Launch

Public launch. Season 1 draft scheduled April 15, 2026. The world's first dedicated boxing fantasy platform goes live.
