The Platform
Everything a Fantasy League Should Be
Built by boxing fans over five seasons of real competition — every feature exists because it makes the game better.
🥊

Season-Long League Play

Draft your squad before the season and score points all year as your fighters compete in real bouts around the world. The longer you plan, the better your team performs.

⚡

Head-to-Head Matchups

The game's signature mechanic. When two managers' fighters meet in the ring, both score double points — turning every elite fight night into a high-stakes fantasy event.

🏆

Belt-Based Scoring

Points for WBA, WBC, IBF and WBO titles mean the biggest champions in boxing are the most valuable assets on the board. Draft the elite — and keep them.

⭐

BoxRec Star Ratings

Fight quality matters. BoxRec's independent star ratings reward managers who draft fighters competing in the sport's biggest, most competitive bouts — not just volume fighters.

🐍

Snake Draft System

A fair, classic snake draft gives every manager a shot at elite talent. Pick order reverses each round — so the first pick in one round is the last in the next. Strategy starts from pick one.

♀️

Women's Boxing Included

Women's boxing is fully integrated across all weight divisions — not an afterthought. Katie Taylor, Amanda Serrano, and the sport's elite women score points just like their male counterparts.

Our Story

Fantasy Fights was founded in 2021 by four friends in Bondi who discovered that no fantasy platform existed for boxing anywhere in the world. Starting with a printed draft sheet and an Excel spreadsheet, the league has run every year since — growing to eight managers, launching a dedicated website, and refining the game through five seasons of real competition. What started as a private league is now the foundation of the world's first public fantasy boxing platform.
