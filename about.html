Where It All Started

A Living Room in Bondi Changed Everything

It was 2021. Four friends gathered in Bondi to watch boxing. One of them — an avid fantasy sports player — had spent years watching fantasy leagues transform fan engagement across football, basketball, and baseball. After discovering that no fantasy sports platform existed for boxing anywhere in the world, he saw a gap that was too obvious to ignore.

Just an hour before fight night, armed with nothing but a printed list of boxers' names and a hastily sketched draft sheet, he pitched the idea. His friends were in. By the end of the night, four teams had been drafted, and Fantasy Fights — without knowing it yet — had been born.

What followed was four years of iteration: Excel spreadsheets evolving into Facebook tracking, then a dedicated website, and now a fully featured platform. Every season brought new features, new managers, and a refined scoring system that rewards genuine boxing knowledge and strategic depth.

The game we built for ourselves turned out to be the game the world was waiting for.

2021 — Season 1

The Foundation Year

Four friends, one draft sheet, and a shared love of boxing. Each manager drafted 19 fighters structured across six divisions plus an "X-Factor" pick. Tracked via Excel and Facebook updates, the league covered an unforgettable year: Kambosos over Lopez, Usyk over Joshua, and the wars between Charlo–Castaño and Chocolatito–Estrada.

2022 — Season 2

Innovation & Expansion

The league expanded to seven teams and introduced the game-changing Head-to-Head scoring system — when two managers' fighters meet in the ring, both earn double points. This single innovation transformed strategy and made marquee fight nights electric. The structure refined to five divisions with a dedicated women's slot (21 fighters per team). Featured Charlo–Castaño II, Joshua–Usyk II, Estrada–Chocolatito III, and the historic Katie Taylor vs. Amanda Serrano.

2023 — Season 3

Digital Transformation

Fantasy Fights entered the digital age with the launch of fantasyfights.live — a dedicated website replacing the Excel and Facebook era. The Platform

Scoring That Rewards Real Knowledge

Fantasy Fights isn't about luck. The scoring system was designed — and refined over five seasons — to reward managers who understand boxing: who the elite fighters are, which belts matter, and how to spot the matchups that produce double points.

Draft 20 fighters spread across five weight divisions using a snake draft. Your roster earns points every time a fighter competes, based on real-world outcomes. Win big fights, hold major belts, and engineer Head-to-Head collisions — that's how champions are made.

The H2H bonus is the game's masterstroke. When two managers' fighters meet in a real bout, both earn double points — turning every elite matchup into a fantasy event in its own right. Drafting the sport's most active and sought-after fighters isn't just smart, it's essential.
