Origin Story
A Living Room Changed Everything
How one printed draft sheet became the world's first fantasy boxing platform.

It was 2021. Four friends gathered in Bondi to watch boxing. One of them — an avid fantasy sports player — had spent years watching fantasy leagues transform fan engagement across football, basketball, and baseball.

After realising that no fantasy sports platform existed for boxing anywhere in the world, he saw a gap too obvious to ignore. Just an hour before fight night, armed with a printed list of fighters and a hastily sketched draft sheet, he pitched the idea. His friends were in.

By the end of the night, four teams had been drafted and Fantasy Fights — without knowing it yet — had been born. What followed was four years of iteration: Excel spreadsheets, then Facebook tracking, then a dedicated website, and now a full platform.

The game we built for ourselves turned out to be the game the world was waiting for.

📋

The Original Draft Sheet

A printed list of fighters and a snake draft that took less than an hour. Season 1 had 19 fighters per team across six divisions — tracked entirely in Excel.

Fantasy Fights isn't about luck. Draft 20 fighters across five weight divisions using a snake draft. Your roster earns points every time a fighter competes, based entirely on real-world outcomes.

The H2H bonus is the game's masterstroke — when two managers' fighters meet in the ring, both earn double points. Drafting the sport's most active, elite fighters isn't just smart, it's essential to winning.

Bigger belts, bigger fights, bigger stars — the scoring mirrors what actually matters in boxing, rewarding the managers who know the sport best.
