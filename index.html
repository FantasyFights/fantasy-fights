Season-Long League Play

Draft your stable before the season and score points all year as your fighters compete in real bouts around the world. The longer you plan, the better your team performs.

Head-to-Head Matchups

The game's signature mechanic. When two managers' fighters meet in the ring, both score double points — turning every elite fight night into a high-stakes fantasy event.

🏆

Belt-Based Scoring

Champions score more. WBA, WBC, IBF and WBO belt holders earn bonus points every time they fight — making the sport's elite the most valuable names in your draft.

⭐

Star Ratings

The quality of a fight counts. Our independent star ratings reward managers who target fighters competing in the sport's biggest, most talked-about bouts — not just the ones with the easiest records.

🐍

Snake Draft System

A fair, classic snake draft gives every manager a shot at elite talent. Pick order reverses each round — strategy starts from pick one.

🥋

Women's Boxing Included

Women's boxing is fully integrated across all weight divisions. Taylor, Serrano, and the sport's elite women score points just like their male counterparts.

The Origin

Boxing has always been about debate. Who beats who. Who's overrated. Who's next. Fantasy Fights turns those debates into competition.

In 2021, four friends in Bondi sat down to do a boxing fantasy draft — and discovered that no platform existed anywhere in the world to run one. So they printed a draft sheet, built a spreadsheet, and did it themselves.

Five seasons later, the league has grown, the game has been refined through real competition, and what started as a private experiment is now the foundation of the world's first public fantasy boxing platform. Everything you see here was built by fans, tested in real seasons, and shaped by the sport itself.
